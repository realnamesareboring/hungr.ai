What is hungrai.ai?

We believe food is deeply connected to emotions. hungrai.ai is the first AI platform that matches your current mood with the perfect dining experience, whether you're cooking at home or exploring restaurants.

🧠

Emotion-First Approach

Unlike traditional food apps that focus on cuisine type or location, we start with how you're feeling and build recommendations around your emotional state.

🎯

Personalized Intelligence

Our AI learns your preferences, dietary restrictions, and past choices to deliver increasingly accurate recommendations that truly understand you.

⚡

Instant Decisions

No more endless scrolling through menus. Get targeted suggestions in under 60 seconds based on your mood, location, budget, and time constraints.

How It Works

Three simple steps to discover your perfect meal, whether you're looking to cook, order in, or dine out.

1

Tell Us Your Mood

Answer a few quick questions about how you're feeling - tired, excited, stressed, celebratory, or somewhere in between. Our AI understands the nuances of your emotional state.

2

Set Your Preferences

Tell us your location, budget range, dietary restrictions, and whether you want to cook at home, order delivery, or explore local restaurants.

3

Get Perfect Matches

Receive curated recommendations that align with your mood, from comfort food recipes for cozy nights to vibrant restaurants for celebratory dinners.

What Makes Us Different

While other platforms focus on ratings and reviews, we focus on the human experience of food and emotion.

🔬

Science-Backed Matching

Our recommendations are based on research connecting emotions to food preferences, nutritional needs, and cultural comfort associations. We understand that spicy food energizes while warm soups comfort.

🌍

Context-Aware Intelligence

We consider weather, time of day, social context, recent activities, and even local events to ensure our recommendations fit perfectly into your life at that exact moment.

🚀

Continuously Learning

Every interaction teaches our AI more about your preferences. Rate a recommendation, and we'll remember what worked for your mood to make future suggestions even better.

About

hungrai.ai was born from the simple observation that our relationship with food is deeply emotional. Created by a team passionate about the intersection of AI, psychology, and culinary experiences, we're building technology that understands the human side of eating.

Our mission is to eliminate decision fatigue around food choices while helping people discover new flavors and experiences that genuinely match their emotional needs.
